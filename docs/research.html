Research

I work in algebraic geometry, with a particular interest in Hodge modules, D-modules, generic vanishing, and Fourier–Mukai transforms on abelian varieties.

Papers & preprints

  1. Generic vanishing for Dolbeault cohomology of mixed Hodge modules on abelian varieties

    Ze Yun  ·  2026  ·  Preprint

    arXivPDF
    About this paper

    This paper studies generic vanishing for the Dolbeault cohomology of mixed Hodge modules. It relates Dolbeault and de Rham cohomology support loci through nonabelian Hodge theory, and applies this to Fourier–Mukai transforms on abelian varieties and their extensions over the family of lambda-connections.

  2. On nefness of the lowest piece of Hodge modules

    Ze Yun  ·  2025, revised 2026  ·  Preprint

    arXivPDF
    About this paper

    This paper gives lower bounds for the degrees of quotient line bundles of the lowest Hodge piece. The bounds depend on boundary monodromy and intersection numbers, explain how nefness can fail outside the unipotent case, and recover Kawamata’s semipositivity theorem for unipotent variations.
